Representation rules

The representation rules are based on a register extract dated 15 September 2022. A more recent extract is not currently available.

The President or a Vice President represents the association in and out of court and is authorised to sign on its behalf. Written declarations require the signature of the President or a Vice President to be valid. Financial transactions (disposal of assets) require the signature of the President and/or a Vice President to be valid.
