Cookie Settings

HUI is transparent: the public website does not use cookies.

Current status

The public HUI website uses no cookies. No tracking cookies, marketing cookies, or analytics cookies are set. No consent management system is active, as no consent-based cookies are used.

Why no cookies?

HUI values transparency and data protection. The public website is designed to work entirely without cookies. Fonts and images are loaded locally — there is no integration of external font providers or CDNs that could set cookies.

The hosting provider (Vercel) processes access data in server log files (IP address, timestamp, requested URL). This processing takes place server-side and not via cookies in the browser.

Prepared for the future

This page is technically prepared for future consent management. If cookies are added as part of the app launch or future features, a central consent management system will be available here, allowing you to adjust your settings at any time.

Cookies requiring consent will only be used once a corresponding consent system is implemented. Until then, no cookies remain active.

Your rights

Since no cookies are used, revoking consent is currently not necessary. If consent-based cookies are added in the future, you can revoke your consent at any time.

Further information can be found in our Privacy Policy.

This page will be updated as soon as the cookie situation changes. No changes are currently foreseeable.
